04 / Quality protocol

Make risk
visible.

Good QA is a decision system. Every test should help the team understand what changed, what matters, and what to do next.

  1. 01

    Model the risk

    Understand the system, the customer, and the cost of failure before choosing coverage.

  2. 02

    Build the signal

    Combine targeted automation, exploratory testing, API checks, and data evidence.

  3. 03

    Close the loop

    Turn failures into fast, specific feedback that improves both the product and the process.
